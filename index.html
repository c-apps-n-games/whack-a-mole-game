Whack-a-Mole 9×9

How to play

Whack as many moles as you can before the 60 seconds run out. Tap or click a mole to hit it.

The moles

  • Regular mole: 1 point.
  • Golden mole: 5 points, but it leaves quickly.
  • Clock mole: adds 3 seconds. Up to 5 can appear per round.
  • Bomb mole: costs 3 points and resets your streak. Leave it alone.

Scoring

Every 5 hits in a row adds 1 more point per hit, up to +5. Tapping an empty hole resets your streak.

Modes

Easy has slower moles and fewer bombs. Chaos has faster moles, more at once and more bombs.

Buttons

Use the icon buttons above the board. Pause (or resume) stops the clock, and the game also pauses when you switch tabs. The circular arrow resets the round. The speaker turns sound on or off. The share icon sends your score after a round.

Rewards

New mole skins unlock at 50, 150, 300 and 500 total whacks. Pick one in the Mole collection. Eight achievements are waiting too, and each one comes with confetti. Hammer skins unlock from whack totals and achievements, and you pick them under Hammers.

Saving

Your best score, skins and achievements are saved in this browser only. Clearing browser data or switching devices starts you over.
